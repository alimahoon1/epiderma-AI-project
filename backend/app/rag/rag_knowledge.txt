Acne Types (6 classes):

Blackheads

Open comedones caused by oxidized sebum.

Treatments: Salicylic acid (2%), benzoyl peroxide (2.5–5%), retinoids, gentle exfoliation.

Dark Spots (Post-inflammatory Hyperpigmentation)

Not active acne. Left after pimples heal.

Treatments: Niacinamide (4–10%), alpha arbutin, vitamin C, sunscreen SPF 50.

Nodules

Severe, painful, deep acne.

Treatments: Dermatologist referral, oral isotretinoin, oral antibiotics.

Papules

Small, red, inflamed bumps.

Treatments: Benzoyl peroxide (2.5%), adapalene 0.1%, salicylic acid.

Pustules

Pimples with white/yellow pus.

Treatments: Benzoyl peroxide (2.5–5%), tea tree oil, avoid popping.

Whiteheads

Closed comedones.

Treatments: Salicylic acid, retinoids, chemical exfoliation (AHA/BHA).

General Acne Advice

Wash face twice daily with gentle cleanser.

Avoid oil-heavy makeup.

Use non-comedogenic products.

Hydrate the skin and avoid scrubbing aggressively.

When to See a Dermatologist

Severe nodules

Scarring acne

Non-stop breakout despite treatment

Highly painful cystic acne
